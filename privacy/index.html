Privacy

This page explains what the contact form collects and what happens with it. There are no cookies and no tracking on this site.

What the form collects

Your name, company, e-mail address and, if you write one, your message. Nothing else is collected and nothing is recorded in the background.

Why

To reply to you and, if we agree on an audit, to prepare it. The data is not used for anything else and is not passed to anyone.

Where it goes

The form sends its content to our own server over an encrypted connection. No third-party form service, mailing tool or analytics is involved.

How long we keep it

Until our exchange ends and 90 days after that. If an audit follows, its contract sets the retention of everything else.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and complain to the data protection authority in your country. Write to us through the form on this site.

Who is responsible

The operator of this site. Its identity and address are stated in the offer and the contract you receive before any audit begins.

Public sources only. Passive methods only. You always know first.
