What is in order

We looked here and found nothing harmful.

1 Registers and authorities
  • The company itself is not in the Insolvency Register.
  • It is not listed as an unreliable VAT payer.
  • It is not on the sanctions lists of the EU, the UN, the United Kingdom or the USA.
  • We did not find the company in the decisions of authorities (consumer protection, competition, personal data protection).
2 Courts
  • Apart from the dispute in finding N-04, no other public court decisions, not even for the connected companies from layer 8.
3 Media
  • Six articles in the last three years, all neutral or positive.
  • The archive of old website versions contains nothing beyond findings N-06 and N-15.
4 Reviews and discussions
  • An average of 4.6 from 212 Google reviews. Without the wave in finding N-07 it would be 4.8.
  • Firmy.cz: 4.7 from 58 ratings. No record on Trustpilot.
5 Search and AI
  • Gemini and Perplexity describe the company correctly.
  • Seven of the first ten Google results for the company name are its own or positive.
6 Technical footprint and security
  • The main website has a valid HTTPS certificate and an up-to-date content management system.
  • The SPF and DKIM records are set up correctly.
  • We found no look-alike domains or fake profiles posing as the company.
7 Words versus deeds
  • The number of completed projects on the website matches the references.
  • The references given can be traced and the customers exist.
8 Connections
  • None of the connected companies is in insolvency or on sanctions lists as of the audit date; the predecessor from finding N-15 has been struck off since 2019.
  • Apart from finding N-18, we found no other tie to a political party, movement or authority.
9 People and processes
  • Job ads from the last two years do not reveal internal systems or the way payments are approved.
  • The website shows only general contact addresses, no named addresses from which an address pattern could be derived.

What we did not do

  • We did not use leaked databases, the dark web or purchased data.
  • We did not search for people by photo and did not use facial recognition.
  • We did not log in to other people's accounts, create fake profiles or pretend to be anyone.
  • We did not actively test your systems. The technical part is passive: only public records of the domain and website. We did not try the exposed key and did not record its value anywhere.
  • We processed people only in their public roles from the registers (managing director, proxy holder, shareholder) and through their other companies, without their private lives and without any criminal matters.
  • We did not check employees or anyone who did not give written consent.

The report shows what was publicly available on the date of the audit and how it can be connected. We searched in rounds until a further round brought nothing new; what we did not find may still exist. Connections between companies, roles and money are signals with evidence, not accusations. The internet and the registers change, so we recommend repeating the check before meeting the investor.
